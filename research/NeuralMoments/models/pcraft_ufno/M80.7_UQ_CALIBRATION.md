# M80.7 — Ensemble Uncertainty Calibration Baseline

Status: independent calibration utilities implemented; CI pending/passed; no calibrated model artifact or solver admission policy has been frozen.

## 1. Purpose

Before importing or reproducing UQNO, establish a transparent ensemble-based baseline for moment and Jacobian uncertainty. This is not an upstream UQNO implementation and it does not infer event validity.

## 2. Implemented functions

Module: spin_pino/uncertainty_calibration.py

- ensemble_moment_uncertainty: normalized RMS ensemble spread over ten moments.
- ensemble_jacobian_uncertainty: normalized RMS ensemble spread over 10x10 Jacobians.
- conformal_ratio_scale: finite-sample upper order statistic of observed error / ensemble spread.
- fit_uncertainty_gate: separate moment and Jacobian calibration multipliers from held-out validation data.
- CalibratedUncertaintyGate.admit: checks event regularity, teacher derivative validity and calibrated moment/Jacobian risk bounds.

## 3. Safety semantics

- Event regularity and derivative validity are separate hard gates. A small uncertainty score never overrides either.
- Calibration must use a held-out validation calibration set, not training data and not the final test set.
- Risk bounds are valid only under the calibration/exchangeability assumptions; distribution shift and unseen geometry families require separate stress tests.
- The gate currently consumes externally supplied ensemble predictions/errors. It does not yet run multiple M80 checkpoints or connect to the active teacher selector.
- The native Newton adapter remains fail-closed unless a caller explicitly supplies a calibrated admission callback.

## 4. Required experiments

1. Train at least 3 independent seeds or a validated ensemble.
2. Compare uncertainty calibration with empirical moment/Jacobian errors by geometry family.
3. Report risk-coverage curves, false derivative acceptance, event recall and OOD behavior.
4. Compare active teacher selection against random/stratified selection at equal exact teacher-call budget.
5. Bind the calibrated gate to M80ExternalMomentEvaluator only after frozen model/data/calibration hashes and event policy are recorded.
