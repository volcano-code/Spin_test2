# M80.5 — Convex Moment Potential Readout Candidate

Date: 2026-11-13  
Status: optional implementation added; CPU mathematical/gradient tests queued; no GPU or trained-quality evidence yet.

## 1. Motivation

The current M72 bounded odd head preserves positive coefficient-scale invariance and the q/-q complement relation, but it does not structurally guarantee a symmetric positive-semidefinite Jacobian. M80.5 adds a separate convex-potential candidate. The existing bounded odd head remains the default and the control.

## 2. Potential construction

Let D be the positive diagonal matrix of coordinate scales and let q_tilde = D q. The geometry-conditioned head predicts K nonnegative amplitudes a_k and K positive-definite correlation matrices C_k:

\[
\Psi_\theta(\tilde q;G)=\sum_{k=1}^{K}a_k(G)\sqrt{\tilde q^\top C_k(G)\tilde q}.
\]

Each C_k is formed as a normalized Gram matrix L_k L_k^T, with a positive diagonal in L_k, then normalized to unit diagonal. Therefore C_k is SPD and has C_{k,ii}=1. The amplitudes are softmax weights times a bounded positive total amplitude.

The editable-domain moment proposal is:

\[
\hat r_D(q)=\frac12 M_D+D\nabla_{\tilde q}\Psi_\theta(Dq;G).
\]

The total response remains r_total = r_fixed + r_D. The fixed material moments are not predicted by the head.

## 3. Structural properties

Because each term is a positive multiple of an SPD norm:
- \Psi is convex and even;
- \Psi is positively 1-homogeneous;
- its gradient is odd and 0-homogeneous;
- its Hessian is symmetric PSD for nonzero q.

Thus, in the smooth surrogate:

\[
\hat r_D(q)+\hat r_D(-q)=M_D,
\]

\[
\hat J_D(q)=D\nabla^2\Psi_\theta(Dq;G)D,
\]

which is symmetric PSD up to floating-point error, and

\[
\hat r_D(\alpha q)=\hat r_D(q),\quad \alpha>0.
\]

## 4. Absolute-moment envelope

For a unit-diagonal PSD correlation matrix, each component of the gradient of sqrt(q^T C q) has magnitude at most one. The implementation caps the sum of amplitudes by:

\[
\sum_k a_k \leq \frac12\min_i \frac{A_i}{D_i},
\]

where A_i is the native absolute-moment bound. Consequently:

\[
|D_i\partial_i\Psi|\leq \frac12 A_i,
\]

so the editable response remains within the same half-design ± half-absolute-moment envelope used by the existing bounded odd head. Exact full/empty quadric bounds remain applied after the response.

## 5. Important limitations

- This is a learned smooth convex potential surrogate, not an exact quadrature formula.
- Smoothness of the surrogate does not certify smoothness of the underlying exact geometry response. Native event classification still controls derivative admission.
- The SPD potential family may be less expressive than the true geometry integral; its value must be established on held-out geometries and Jacobian tails.
- The amplitude envelope is a structural bound, not a calibrated uncertainty certificate.
- The current implementation uses AD to obtain the first derivative; HVP and solver integration remain separate gates.

## 7. Current implementation boundary

- Optional config flag: convex_potential_enabled.
- Default M80.1/M80.4 presets remain on the original bounded odd head.
- New large_convex, xl_convex and large_ms_convex presets are opt-in.
- The convex head is not yet promoted, not GPU-preflighted and not used by Newton.
