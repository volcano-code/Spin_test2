# M80.6 — Cost-aware Active Teacher Query Selection

Status: deterministic selection utility implemented; CPU tests pending/passed in CI; calibrated UQ producer and Cherchi integration are not yet implemented.

## Implemented utility

Module: spin_pino/active_query_selection.py

It accepts candidate queries with:
- calibrated uncertainty in [0,1];
- solver sensitivity in [0,1];
- coverage novelty in [0,1];
- signed event margin;
- predicted derivative-valid flag;
- estimated teacher-call cost;
- source geometry group.

It ranks queries using a weighted acquisition score divided by estimated teacher cost, then greedily selects under a fixed cost budget. Optional per-source-group caps prevent one geometry from consuming the entire budget.

## Query equivalence

The deduplication key normalizes q by a positive Euclidean norm. Therefore q and alpha*q for alpha>0 are treated as equivalent, while q and -q remain distinct because they represent complementary material domains under the M72 contract.

## Purposes

- mixed: balance uncertainty, solver sensitivity, coverage novelty and event proximity.
- derivative: exclude predicted-invalid and event-near queries; exact teacher audit is still required.
- event: prioritize queries near event boundaries for event labels. These are not automatically valid Jacobian training samples.

## Safety boundary

This module is not a UQ predictor and does not certify derivative validity. It consumes scores from a separately calibrated predictor/auditor. Cherchi remains the authoritative teacher. A selected candidate must pass exact teacher evaluation and the formal dataset admission process before becoming a training label.
